# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Designers and developers collecting mobile webpages and app screens, then arranging editable designs and explicit prototype interactions on their computer in Figma. The phone is used one-handed while reviewing another app or a mobile webpage.

## Product Purpose

Capture chosen states, retain them reliably on the phone, transfer inspectable materials to the computer, and distinguish material receipt, conversion, and actual Figma import. Android and iOS have equal priority.

## Brand Personality

Simple, precise, dependable. Continue the desktop's quiet working interface and the existing Expo project's blue accent. Chinese task-focused labels, native system typography, minimal decoration.

## Anti-references

No decorative dashboards, invented statistics, nested cards, giant marketing headings, or dense desktop panels squeezed onto a phone. Do not claim a screenshot or OCR overlay is fully editable.

## Design Principles

1. Preserve saved work through failures and cancellations.
2. Show actual capabilities and distinct task receipts.
3. Make the primary capture action obvious and progressive controls discoverable.
4. Keep sensitive redactions effective in the actual shared assets.
5. Let the user choose states and prototype actions explicitly.
6. Use direct Chinese action labels: add screens, choose screens, send to computer. Keep file export, numeric editing and diagnostics out of the primary flow. The user explicitly prefers a simpler interface and functional progress over extensive acceptance work.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Respect system font scaling, safe areas, system back navigation, light/dark settings and reduced motion. Touch targets are at least 48 logical units; controls have readable labels; states use words as well as color. Derived from docs/APP-PLAN.md and docs/DESIGN.md, not a new user interview.
